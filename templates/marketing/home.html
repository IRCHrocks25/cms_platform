Why Katek CMS

A generic site builder gives away the layout. Katek CMS does not.

Hand a client their website without handing over the design. They control the content. You keep the structure exactly as shipped.

Content without structural access

Clients update headlines, paragraphs, images, buttons, links and brand colours. They cannot move a section, delete one or break the layout.

A plain editor, not a page builder

Katek CMS builds the editing screen section by section, with labels clients understand. There is nothing to drag and no page builder to learn.

A live preview before publish

Clients see edits beside the controls, preview the site on phone, tablet and desktop, then publish on their own schedule.

A safety net for every change

Edits save as the client types. The last ten versions remain available so a previous version can be restored when they change their mind.
